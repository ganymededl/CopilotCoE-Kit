# Champions toolkit
> For department champions: what you do and don't do, a monthly rhythm, a 10-minute demo script, prompts of the week, and where to send questions.

Everything a department champion needs to help colleagues use Copilot Chat safely and well, in about two hours a month.

## What champions do, and don't do

| Champions do | Champions don't |
|---|---|
| Show colleagues useful prompts for their real work | Approve access, licenses, or new AI tools |
| Run short demos and office hours | Handle data or safety incidents |
| Collect questions, feedback, and success stories | Answer policy questions the organization hasn't answered |
| Point people to the right organizational guidance | Review anyone's use of AI or report on individuals |

## Your monthly rhythm

| Week | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post the prompt of the week in your department's Teams chat or channel | 10 min |
| 2 | Give a 10-minute demo at a department meeting (script below) | 30 min, including preparation |
| 3 | Hold 30 minutes of open office hours | 30 min |
| 4 | Share your department's top questions and one story idea in the private champions space | 20 min |

Each month, tell the CoE if your details or office hours change, so it can update your row and its **Last verified** date in the Department Champions directory.

## 10-minute department demo

Use the same five steps each time, and change the example to suit your department.

1. **Why it matters (1 min).** Confirm Copilot Chat is available with your work account. Today's example explores a task this department does every week; record any time saved rather than assuming it.
2. **Sign in and the green shield (1 min).** Show copilot.cloud.microsoft, work account sign-in, and the green shield.
3. **One real task (4 min).** Run one prompt from the [Prompt library](Guide-Prompt-Library.aspx) on Public material from your department. Show the first result, one follow-up prompt, and what you changed by hand.
4. **The data rule (2 min).** Public: yes. Internal: only with the green shield, and only if the organization's rules allow it. Confidential: no, unless the organization has approved that use. Point to the [Is this OK to use?](Guide-Is-This-OK.aspx) check.
5. **Where to go next (2 min).** Learning Series, Prompt library, your office hours, and Ask Us Anything.

Don't demonstrate with real resident, case, personnel, or other not-public information, even on your own screen.

## Office hours post (copy and edit)

**Copilot office hours: [day], [time], [location or Teams link]**

Bring a task you'd like to try with Copilot Chat: an email, a summary of a public document, a briefing, a notice. We'll work through it together. Please don't bring confidential information such as case files or personnel files. Can't make it? Post your question in Ask Us Anything.

## Prompts of the week (one quarter)

Each is tagged with the most sensitive data level it needs. Ask the CoE to confirm the tags match the organization's data rules.

| Week | Prompt | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Rewrite this public notice so a resident understands it in under a minute. Keep every date: [paste]." | Public |
| 2 | "Turn these notes I wrote into an action list with owners and dates. Don't add anything: [paste notes]." | Internal OK |
| 3 | "Explain [concept] three ways: an analogy, a worked example, and a common mistake." | Public |
| 4 | "Draft three subject lines for this announcement: formal, friendly, and urgent: [paste]." | Public |
| 5 | "Review this text for plain language and accessibility, and suggest alt text for these images: [describe images]." | Public |
| 6 | "Compare these two options in a table of benefits, risks, and open questions: [paste options]." | Internal OK |
| 7 | "Draft a polite, 120-word reply declining [request] and suggesting [alternative]." | Internal OK |
| 8 | "Write five practice questions on [topic] in order of difficulty, with answers." | Public |

## When to send someone elsewhere

| The question is about... | Send them to... |
|---|---|
| Sign-in, access, devices, or errors | The {{Link:ServiceDesk|IT service desk}} |
| Something Copilot showed them that they shouldn't see, or confidential data entered into AI | {{Link:DataSafetyIncident|Report a data or safety incident}}. Stop using the affected content; do not collect or share sensitive details. |
| Whether a use is allowed | The [Is this OK to use?](Guide-Is-This-OK.aspx) check, then the IT service desk if still unclear |
| Public records or data practices questions | The agency's records or data practices officer |
| Paid Copilot or a pilot | The [Service Catalog and Support](CoE-Service-Catalog-and-Support.aspx) page |
| Buying another AI tool | The {{Link:TechnologyRequest|technology request process}} |
| Accessibility barriers | The agency's ADA coordinator or the CoE |

{{IfRouteMissing:DataSafetyIncident|If an incident route is **not configured**, use an existing approved organizational incident route if known, or ask for the correct route without sensitive details.}} Do not post those details in Teams, general feedback or email; the champions space does not submit a report or replace incident handling.

## Getting started as a new champion

1. Complete the Copilot Chat basics session in the Learning Series.
2. Read the [Governance and Responsible AI](CoE-Governance-and-Responsible-AI.aspx) page and the [Is this OK to use?](Guide-Is-This-OK.aspx) check.
3. Try every prompt in [Start in 10 minutes](Guide-Start-in-10-Minutes.aspx) for your own role.
4. Confirm your department, backup champion, and office hours with the CoE.
5. Ask the CoE to add you to the private champions space in Teams (a private channel or group chat), where champions compare notes and the CoE shares material before it goes out. Keep department questions there, not in Ask Us Anything, which every Team member can read.

## Recognition

Each quarter the CoE thanks champions publicly in the General channel and features stories they helped bring forward.

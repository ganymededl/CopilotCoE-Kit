# Is this OK to use?
> A one-minute check before you use Copilot or any other AI tool for organizational work: four questions and a table of common cases.

This check summarizes your organization's {{Link:AIGuidelines|AI guidelines}}. Those guidelines are the authority if anything here differs.{{IfRouteMissing:AIGuidelines| If the policy route is not configured, these examples do not authorize use; confirm the rules before using non-public information.}}

**Something already went wrong?** {{Link:DataSafetyIncident|Report a data or safety incident}}. Stop using the affected content and do not copy or share it. Do not post sensitive details in Teams, general feedback or email.{{IfRouteMissing:DataSafetyIncident| If the link says **route not configured**, use an existing approved organizational incident route if you know it, or ask for the correct route without sharing sensitive details; this page does not submit a report.}}

## Answer four questions

**1. What is the most sensitive information you will paste, upload, or have open?**

| If it is... | Then... |
|---|---|
| High (protected or regulated): personal information about residents or employees, case files, health, tax, criminal justice, personnel, or financial information, or anything protected by law or contract | **Don't**, unless your organization has approved that specific use. |
| Moderate (not public): day-to-day agency business such as planning notes, draft procedures, or working drafts | Go to question 2. |
| Low (public): published or public information | Go to question 3. Any AI tool your organization allows is fine. |

**2. Are you signed in with your work account, with the enterprise data protection indicator showing?**

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Yes | Go to question 3. |
| No, or you're using a personal account or another AI tool | **Don't** use Moderate (not public) information. Use Low (public) information only, or sign in with your work account. |

**3. Will the result be used to decide something about a person?** For example, eligibility for a benefit or service, a hire, discipline, an enforcement action, or an accommodation.

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Yes | **Don't** let AI make or shape that decision. A qualified person decides. |
| No | Go to question 4. |

**4. Will you share or publish the result?**

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Yes | **Go ahead.** Verify facts and citations, and disclose AI help following your organization's guidance. |
| No, it's for your own drafting | **Go ahead.** Still check it before relying on it. |

## Common cases

These are starting points. Use your organization's own ratings where they exist.

| What you want to do | Usually | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Refine something you wrote | Go ahead | Remove identifying information that isn't already public, and check the meaning didn't change. |
| Brainstorm a list of ideas | Go ahead | Check the ideas before you act on them. |
| Write an event announcement | Go ahead | Check every date, time, and location in the final version. |
| Summarize a document | Depends on the document | Check the document's data level first. Read the original before making any important decision. |
| Analyze data or survey responses | Depends on the data | Remove identifying information first, even in a protected tool. Ask IT if you're unsure. |
| Draft a policy or procedure | Ask first | A subject matter expert must review it before it is used. |
| Draft wording for a performance review | Ask first | The judgments must be yours. Don't include High (protected or regulated) information. |
| Screen job applicants, evaluate bids, or decide eligibility | Don't | AI can introduce bias, and these are decisions about people or contracts. |
| Record or summarize a meeting with an AI notetaker | Ask first | Use only notetakers your organization has approved, and never when confidential matters are discussed. |
| Paste case files or residents' personal information | Don't | Not without explicit organizational approval for that use. |
| Buy or install another AI tool | Ask first | Follow the {{Link:TechnologyRequest|technology request process}} before you buy or use it. |

## Still not sure?

- Ask your [department champion](Meet-your-Champions.aspx) about everyday uses.
- Contact the {{Link:ServiceDesk|IT service desk}} with a data question.
- If something has already gone wrong, use the separate incident route above, not general feedback or an ordinary support request.

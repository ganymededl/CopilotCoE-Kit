# Who gets what: Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot

> Which Copilot you have, what it can use, and how to tell. Start here before training or support.

Sign in to the Microsoft Copilot app with your work account. What it can do depends on your Microsoft 365 plan and whether your organization has assigned you a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. The app label helps you identify the experience; it does not rename the paid product or license.

| | Copilot Chat (Basic) | Copilot (Basic) | Microsoft 365 Copilot (Copilot Premium in the app) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who typically has it | Employees with a work account on an eligible Microsoft 365 plan | Users whose Microsoft 365 plan includes this tier (check the label) | People your organization assigns a paid Copilot license |
| What it can use | Web information and files you upload to the chat | Web information and files you upload | Web information plus your work email, files, chats, and meetings you can already open |
| Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote | No | Standard access | Priority access to the full experience |
| Agents | Agents that use web information; pay-as-you-go agents if your organization enables them | Same as Copilot Chat | Agents that can also use organizational information |
| Extra cost to the user | None | None | Licensed by your organization |

## What this means in practice

- **Copilot Chat** is a secure AI chat for drafting, summarizing, explaining, and brainstorming. It works with what you type or upload and information from the web. In some apps, such as Outlook, it can also read the email you have open and may use your inbox and calendar - it does not add the in-app authoring experience inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or OneNote (the table's "No" row above); that embedded experience is part of Microsoft 365 Copilot. It does not search across all your work files the way Microsoft 365 Copilot does. Don't open confidential files or email and then ask Copilot about them.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** can find and summarize organizational information you already have access to. It never gives you access you did not already have, so permission problems on SharePoint sites and Teams files matter more.
- Your prompts and responses are protected by enterprise data protection when you sign in with your work account. Do not use a personal Microsoft account or consumer AI service for organizational work.

## How to check which one you have

1. Go to copilot.cloud.microsoft and sign in with your work account.
2. Look for the label near the top: Copilot Chat (Basic), Copilot (Basic), or Copilot (Premium). These are app labels; the paid product and license are called Microsoft 365 Copilot.
3. If you see a consumer sign-in page or no enterprise data protection indicator, you are not signed in with your work account.

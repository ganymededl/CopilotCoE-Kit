# Prompt library by role
> Ready-to-adapt prompts for staff, supervisors, IT, communications, and leaders. Replace the [brackets] and use only data you are allowed to use.

Copy a prompt, replace every [bracket], and review the result before you use it. Do not paste residents' personal information, case files, health, tax, criminal justice, personnel, or other not-public information unless your organization has approved Copilot for that data. Share prompts that work well in the Share Your Prompt channel.

## Everyday work

### Clear communication

"Rewrite this public notice in plain language so a resident can understand it in under a minute: [paste notice]. Keep all dates, requirements and commitments unchanged. List any unclear points for me to review."

### Action list from your notes

"Turn these public or synthetic notes into an action list with owners and dates: [paste notes]. Use only the supplied information. Mark missing owners and dates as not specified; do not invent them."

### Reply to a constituent

"Draft a courteous reply to this public inquiry: [paste the question with names and personal details removed]. Use only the facts in [paste approved public information]. Point to the official web page for details and flag anything that needs a subject matter expert."

### Plain-language rewrite

"Rewrite this paragraph from a public rule or procedure at an 8th-grade reading level without changing its meaning: [paste text]. List any terms you could not simplify safely."

## Supervisors and program managers

### Meeting summary

"Summarize these non-sensitive meeting notes into decisions, open questions, and next steps with owners and due dates: [paste notes]. Do not add anything that is not in the notes."

### Briefing

"Prepare a one-page briefing on [topic] for [audience] with background, options, a recommendation, and what we still need to know. Use only these sources: [paste or attach]."

### Procedure checklist

"Turn this approved procedure into a step-by-step checklist for new team members. Keep every requirement and deadline: [paste procedure]."

## Policy and program staff

### Policy draft

"Draft a first version of a [process] procedure using these requirements: [paste]. Flag anything that needs legal, HR, or data practices review."

### Process map

"List the steps, hand-offs, and delays in this process description, then suggest three improvements: [paste description]."

### Compare public guidance

"Compare these two public documents on [topic] in a table: what each requires, where they differ, and questions for our legal or policy team: [attach documents]."

## Champions: share what works

### Record a reviewed prompt pattern

"Organize these non-sensitive practice notes into a reusable prompt pattern: [paste notes]. Include the task, source limits, prompt, manual corrections, actual time including review, result and limitations. Mark anything not recorded as not measured; do not invent savings or approvals."

## IT and service desk

### Knowledge article

"Using this public Microsoft documentation, write a step-by-step help article for [task] with a short troubleshooting section: [paste or link]."

### Ticket pattern summary

"From these anonymized ticket summaries, group the issues into themes and suggest one knowledge article per theme: [paste without names]."

### Change notice

"Write a short, friendly notice announcing [change] on [date], what users must do, and where to get help."

## Communications

### Public announcement

"Write three versions of a 50-word announcement for [program or event] aimed at [audience]: formal, plain-language, and social media. Keep every date and eligibility rule unchanged."

### Accessibility pass

"Review this text for plain language and accessibility, and suggest alt text for these images: [describe images]."

## Data and analysis (Excel)

### Explain the data

"Describe the trends in this table, the three largest changes, and any values that look like errors."

### Build a formula

"Write an Excel formula that [goal] using columns [A, B, C], and explain how it works."

## Leadership and planning

### Options memo

"Compare these options for [decision] in a table of benefits, risks, cost drivers, and open questions: [paste options]."

### Budget narrative

"Draft a plain-language narrative for this public budget request: [paste figures and purpose]. Do not change any number; list assumptions I should confirm."

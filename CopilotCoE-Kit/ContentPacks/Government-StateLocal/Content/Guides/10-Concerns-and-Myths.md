# Questions and concerns about AI
> Honest answers to the concerns people raise most often: training on your data, accuracy, bias, public records, jobs, the environment, and more.

These are the concerns people raise most often about Copilot. If yours isn't here, ask in Ask Us Anything.

## "Microsoft trains its AI on what I type."

Not when you sign in with your work account. With the enterprise data protection indicator showing, enterprise data protection applies: Microsoft doesn't use your prompts and responses to train its foundation models. With a personal account or a consumer AI tool, those protections don't apply, so use Public information only. See [Enterprise data protection](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/enterprise-data-protection).

## "Copilot can see all my files and email."

Copilot Chat works with what you type or upload and information from the web. In Outlook it may also use the email you have open, your inbox, calendar, and meetings - that in-app reading does not extend to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or OneNote. It can never open anything you don't already have access to. The paid Microsoft Copilot license adds the in-app experience inside Word/Excel/PowerPoint/OneNote and can search across the files and mail you can access; see [Who gets what](Guide-Who-Gets-What.aspx) for what your organization offers. Exact in-app availability varies by tenant configuration and rollout status per Microsoft's own documentation, so confirm your organization's current state (including any GCC/GCC-Moderate differences) rather than relying on this alone.

## "What I type into Copilot is never a public record."

Don't assume that. Prompts, responses, and drafts created with Copilot may be government data subject to public-records and records-retention rules, like other work email and documents. Follow your agency's records guidance, and ask your records or data practices officer when unsure.

## "If Copilot cites a source, the answer is right."

No. Copilot can be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong, and a citation or confident tone is not proof. Open the source and check that it says what Copilot claims. You stay responsible for anything you share or decide.

## "AI is unbiased because it's a computer."

AI can reflect bias in the data it learned from. That's why decisions about people, such as screening job applicants, stay with a qualified person. See [Is this OK to use?](Guide-Is-This-OK.aspx).

## "AI is going to replace my job."

The organization's approach keeps people responsible for decisions and for reviewing AI output. The CoE's focus is helping people spend less time on routine drafting and more on work that needs their judgment. If you have concerns about how AI affects your role, raise them with your supervisor, or tell the CoE through the [Give Copilot Feedback](Give-Feedback.aspx) page so it can bring common concerns to leadership.

## "What about the environmental cost?"

It's a fair concern. Large AI models use significant energy and water. A practical step: use AI where it saves real effort, not for tasks a quick search or your own knowledge handles. 

## "Can I paste copyrighted material or other people's work?"

Be careful. Intellectual property and copyright are real risks with generative AI. Don't paste colleagues' or vendors' work without permission. Check that AI output doesn't reproduce someone else's work before you publish it.

## "People who can't use AI tools will be left behind."

The CoE recommends that an AI tool is never the only way to complete a required task. If a tool doesn't work with your assistive technology or device, ask your supervisor or your agency's ADA coordinator for an equivalent option.

## "I'll get in trouble just for trying it."

No. Refining your own writing, brainstorming, and drafting announcements from public information are low-risk ways to start. Use your work account, check for the enterprise data protection indicator, and use the [Is this OK to use?](Guide-Is-This-OK.aspx) check when you're unsure.

## "Other AI tools are better. Why Copilot?"

Copilot Chat with your work account includes enterprise data protection, which consumer AI tools don't. Other tools may be allowed for Public information only, and some may not be allowed at all. Check the {{Link:AIGuidelines|AI guidelines}} for the current list. Before buying or installing another AI tool, follow the {{Link:TechnologyRequest|technology request process}} so it is reviewed for security, accessibility, and legal considerations.

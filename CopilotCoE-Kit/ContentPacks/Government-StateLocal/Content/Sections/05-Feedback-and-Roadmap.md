## Tell us what you need

- Post ideas and questions in this channel. Reply to an existing post instead of starting a new one when the topic is the same.
- Use the **Give Copilot Feedback** page for structured, non-sensitive feedback.
- Product suggestions can also go to Microsoft through the thumbs-up or thumbs-down control in Copilot when your administrator has enabled it.
- Never post confidential information. Data or safety concerns belong in the separate incident route above, not this channel or general feedback.

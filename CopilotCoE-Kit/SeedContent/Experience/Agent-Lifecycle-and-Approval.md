# Agent lifecycle and approval
> A proposed review path for makers, the CoE and IT. Your organization must confirm its actual authority, policy and admin-center configuration before adopting it.

## Risk tiers

| Tier | Typical case | Before wider use |
|---|---|---|
| Low risk | Personal or team agent using public or approved non-sensitive sources | Complete training, keep ownership and permitted sharing within your team |
| CoE review | Agent shared beyond a team, used by a wide audience or based on mixed SharePoint content | Submit the purpose, owner, sources, audience and test results for CoE review |
| Formal approval | Restricted data, regulated sources or actions in other systems | Security and data-owner review plus the required IT approval; do not publish while pending |
| Not permitted | Profiles, ranks or monitors people, or makes consequential decisions about them | Do not build or publish |

These are starter tiers, not authorization to use protected records or bypass local policy.

## From proposal to publication

1. **Maker submits.** Choose an approved build surface, document purpose, owner and backup, sources, audience, data class, connectors, actions and positive/negative access tests. Propose the agent through the [Agent Showcase](CoE-Agent-Showcase.aspx); do not put sensitive evidence in the catalog.
2. **CoE and Showcase reviewers assess.** Confirm the risk tier, sources, name and description, support owner and local data-protection rules. Hold or reject unsafe proposals. Showcase review of the listing is not IT authorization to deploy the agent.
3. **IT approves in the admin center when required.** The designated admin checks the agent's tenant availability, sharing, connectors, DLP policies, environment, billing and the applicable admin-center approval and registry state. A low-risk personal agent need not imply an IT ticket; broader publication must wait for the actual required admin approval.
4. **Publish to the approved audience.** Only after required CoE, data-owner and IT decisions are complete should the agent be shared. The Showcase reviewer approves its listing; Featured is an editorial designation, not a platform permission. Listing an agent does not grant source access.

## Maintain and retire

Review shared agents at least every six months and whenever sources, actions, owner or audience change. Retire an agent when its owner leaves, its sources are no longer approved or its value ends. Review Showcase edits and retirement through the existing content-approval process; also revoke platform access in the admin center when applicable.

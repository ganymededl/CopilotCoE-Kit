## What to include

- What task you tried, using no sensitive details
- What helped or did not work
- What training or resource would help next

**Copyable prompt:** I tried [task]. It helped with [result]. I still need help with [issue]. A resource I would use is [resource].

## Report a concern instead of posting it

If Copilot showed you something you should not see, produced harmful or discriminatory output, or confidential information was entered into any AI tool:

- Stop using the affected content, and do not copy or share it.
- Use the separate incident route above, not ordinary IT requests or general feedback.
- Do not post sensitive details in Teams, general feedback or email.
- For academic-integrity questions, follow your course and department procedures.

A public channel is visible to all of its members. Product suggestions can also be sent with the thumbs-up/down feedback control inside Copilot when your administrator has enabled it.

# Stay current and keep learning

> Where to find what's new in Copilot, self-paced learning, and the champion community.

Copilot changes every month. The CoE uses these sources to decide what to announce, train, or hold back.

## What's new

- [Copilot release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes): monthly summary of new features.
- [Microsoft 365 Roadmap](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap): planned and rolling-out features.
- Message center in the Microsoft 365 admin center: tenant-specific change notices for administrators. See [Message center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/manage/message-center?view=o365-worldwide).

The CoE reviews these sources monthly and posts a short "what changed for the organization" note in the General channel.

# Students and AI

> What students can use, what instructors decide, and how to use Copilot to learn rather than to replace learning.

## Can I use Copilot?

Yes. Students 13 and older can use Copilot Chat at no extra cost by signing in with their school account at copilot.cloud.microsoft. Using that account matters: it keeps your conversations under your organization's data protections. Do not use a personal account for coursework.

## Your instructor decides how AI is used in each course

Rules differ from course to course. One course may not allow AI at all; another may allow it for brainstorming with a citation; another may build it into assignments. Always:

- Read the AI statement in your syllabus or course site.
- Ask your instructor when it is unclear, before you start.
- Say how you used AI when your instructor asks you to, for example: "I used Copilot Chat to generate practice questions and to check my outline."
- Keep your notes and drafts so you can show your own work.

Submitting AI-generated work as your own when it is not allowed is an academic-integrity issue.

## Study and Learn

The **Study and Learn** agent in Copilot is designed for learning. It asks questions, gives hints, and builds flashcards and quizzes from your own notes and slides instead of simply giving answers. Find it in the left pane of the Copilot app.

## Use it well

1. Ask Copilot to explain a concept in a different way or with an example.
2. Paste your own notes and ask for a practice quiz.
3. Ask for feedback on the structure of your draft, then revise it yourself.
4. Check facts, dates, and citations against your textbook or library sources. Copilot can be wrong.

## Protect yourself and others

- Do not paste other people's personal information, classmates' work, or anything your instructor asked you to keep private.
- Do not enter passwords, financial details, or health information.
- If Copilot shows you something that looks private or belongs to someone else, stop using the affected content and do not copy or share it. {{Link:DataSafetyIncident|Report a data or safety incident}}. ## Accessibility and alternatives

The CoE recommends that an AI tool is never the only way to complete a required activity, and asks instructors to offer an equivalent option. If a tool does not work with your assistive technology or device, ask your instructor or your organization's disability services office.

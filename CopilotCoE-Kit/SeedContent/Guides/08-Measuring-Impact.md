# Measuring Copilot impact
> How the CoE measures value: time saved, efficiency, quality, and progress on organizational goals, with honest methods and no invented numbers.

The CoE reports results the organization can verify. Start with a baseline, measure the same task the same way after Copilot is in use, and publish ranges rather than single precise numbers.

## Four kinds of impact

| Category | What to measure | How to measure it | Example target to set locally |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | Minutes saved per task or per person each week | Short pulse survey plus timed samples of common tasks | A cycle-time reduction the organization agrees is meaningful |
| Efficiency | Output per hour, backlog, turnaround time | Before-and-after process data from the system that tracks the work | Shorter turnaround on a named process |
| Quality | Accuracy, consistency, accessibility, satisfaction | A simple quality rubric scored by reviewers; satisfaction survey | No drop in quality; fewer revisions |
| Outcomes | Progress on organizational priorities (student support, research, service capacity) | Existing institutional measures, reported by their owners | Movement on one or two existing priority measures |

## Usage signals (available to administrators)

- **Copilot usage reports** in the Microsoft 365 admin center: active users and usage by app.
- **Copilot Analytics / Viva Insights** (when licensed): adoption and impact dashboards by group, with privacy thresholds.
- **CoE signals:** attendance at Learning Series sessions, prompts shared, stories submitted, and questions in Ask Us Anything.

Usage shows adoption, not value. Pair it with the time, quality, and outcome measures above.

## A simple measurement plan

1. **Pick 3 to 5 common tasks** per audience (for example: routine email drafts for staff, rubric drafts for faculty, knowledge articles for the service desk).
2. **Baseline** how long each takes today, from a short survey or a few timed samples.
3. **Train and enable** the group through the Learning Series and the Prompt library.
4. **Re-measure after 4 to 6 weeks** using the same method.
5. **Report ranges and stories** together: the numbers, plus one or two Success Stories that show what changed.

## Worksheet for estimating value

Use this only with the organization's own measured inputs.

| Input | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Minutes saved per task (measured) | Your baseline and re-measure |
| Times the task is done per week | Process owner |
| Number of people doing the task with Copilot | License or usage report |
| Working weeks per year | HR calendar |

Hours saved per year = minutes saved x times per week x people x weeks / 60. Report it as a range and state the assumptions.

## Reporting cadence

- **Monthly:** usage, training attendance, and open feedback themes to the program team.
- **Quarterly:** time, quality, and outcome results plus stories to leadership, with a recommendation to expand, adjust, or stop.

# Who gets what: Copilot Chat and paid Copilot

> Which Copilot you have, what it can use, and how to tell. Start here before training or support.

Sign in to Copilot with your work or school account. What Copilot can do depends on your Microsoft 365 plan and whether your organization has assigned you a paid Copilot license. The label in the Copilot app, and in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, tells you which experience you have.

| | Copilot Chat (Basic) | Copilot (Basic) | Copilot (Premium) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who typically has it | Faculty, staff, and students 13+ on eligible education plans | Users whose Microsoft 365 plan includes this tier (check the label) | People your organization assigns a paid Copilot license |
| What it can use | Web information and files you upload to the chat | Web information and files you upload | Web information plus your work or school email, files, chats, and meetings you can already open |
| Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote | No | Standard access | Priority access to the full experience |
| Agents | Agents that use web information; pay-as-you-go agents if your organization enables them | Same as Copilot Chat | Agents that can also use organizational information |
| Extra cost to the user | None | None | Licensed by your organization |

## What this means in practice

- **Copilot Chat** is a secure AI chat for drafting, summarizing, explaining, and brainstorming. It works with what you type or upload, information from the web, and the file or email you have open when you use it inside a Microsoft 365 app; in Outlook it may also use your inbox and calendar. It does not search across all your work or school files the way Premium does. Don't open confidential files or email and then ask Copilot about them.
- **Premium Copilot** can find and summarize organizational information you already have access to. It never gives you access you did not already have, so permission problems on SharePoint sites and Teams files matter more.
- Your prompts and responses are protected by enterprise data protection when you sign in with your work or school account. Do not use a personal Microsoft account or consumer AI service for organizational work.

## How to check which one you have

1. Go to copilot.cloud.microsoft and sign in with your work or school account.
2. Look for the label near the top: Copilot Chat (Basic), Copilot (Basic), or Copilot (Premium).
3. If you see a consumer sign-in page or no shield icon, you are not signed in with your work or school account.

## Requesting a paid license

Paid licenses are allocated according to your organization's priorities and budget. To ask about one, use the Service Catalog and Support page.

SUCCESS STORY VIDEOS - SUBMISSION CHECKLIST

Before you submit a video (keep drafts restricted to reviewers):
  [ ] About 2 minutes, one task, showing the prompt, the result, and your edits
  [ ] No student names, grades, ID numbers, health or other confidential data on screen
  [ ] Everyone who appears or is heard has agreed to share it with the intended community
  [ ] Captions turned on and corrected in the video player
  [ ] A row added to the Copilot Success Stories list with Status = Submitted
  [ ] Results reflect actual measurement, including review; use "not measured" when unknown

The CoE team reviews the story, video, consent, captions, evidence and file
permissions, then selects Approve. Status stays Submitted for active stories;
legacy Published is supported for compatibility, not a separate publication step.

Revisions and retirement requests also need approval. Readers see the last
approved version while an edit is pending. To propose removal, set Status to
Retired and request approval. Share files only with the approved audience;
list approval does not restrict a broadly shared video file.

# Training and Enablement
> Role-based learning paths, live sessions, and self-paced resources for faculty, staff, and students.

## Learn, practice, check

Start with Step 1 for your role. Open **Learn**, then try **Practice** using public or made-up information you are allowed to use.

Select a step's name for its practice task, access requirements and **What good looks like**. Review the result before relying on it. Times are suggested exercise lengths. Your organization decides which training is required.

Practice links open a named prompt in the [CoE prompt guide](Guide-Prompt-Library.aspx), or your organization's prompt library when one is provided.

The list does not record personal completion. Use your organization's learning system when one is provided.

## Story list

The **Copilot Success Stories** list tracks each story: audience, task, measured results and video link.

Changes to a published story are reviewed before they appear. To take a story down, contact the CoE team.

## Share your own story (about 2 minutes of video)

1. **Pick one task.** For example: drafting a lab rubric, turning your own bullet notes into an action list, or building a study guide from your own notes.
2. **Record your screen.** Use an organization-approved recording tool, such as Clipchamp where available. Show the prompt, the result, and what you changed.
3. **Keep it safe.** Show no student names, grades, ID numbers, health information, or other confidential data. Use sample or public content only.
4. **Get consent.** Everyone who appears or is heard in the video must agree to it being shared with the intended community.
5. **Add captions.** Turn on automatic captions in the video player and correct them, so the video meets accessibility expectations.
6. **Submit for review.** Follow the CoE's approved submission route and keep draft videos restricted to reviewers. Add a row to the Copilot Success Stories list with Status set to **Submitted**. The CoE reviews the content, consent, captions, evidence and file permissions, then selects **Approve** when ready. Share the video with the approved audience only after review.

## Story prompt to copy

I used Copilot to [task] for [audience]. My measured result, including review and correction, was [result, or "not measured"]. The prompt that worked best was [prompt]. I always check [what you verify]. The limitations were [limitations].
